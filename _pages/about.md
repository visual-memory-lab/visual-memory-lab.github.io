I am an Assistant Professor working in the School of Psychology at the University of Nottingham. Previously, I worked as a postdoctoral research fellow at the Department of Experimental Psychology at the University of Oxford and in the Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute at UC Berkeley. I completed my PhD in Neuroscience with Kia Nobre and Mark Stokes at the University of Oxford.

I am interested in understanding the constraints placed on our behaviour by the limitations of working memory, and how these can be circumvented through attention, learning, and external interventions like brain stimulations.

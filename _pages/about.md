The Visual Memory Lab in the School of Psychology at the University of Nottingham investigates the constraints placed on our behaviour by the limitations of working memory, and how these can be circumvented through attention, learning, and external interventions like brain stimulations. We use neuroimaging (mainly EEG, MEG, and intracranial EEG) and brain stimulation to investigate how memories are represented in the brain and how these representations are shaped to attain our goals. We also investigate how memory changes across the lifespan, and how declining memory may be restored.
